ACL·SCAN's Precision Diagnostics

Our mission is to detect ACL tears with the most precise diagnostic insights.

01
Grad-CAM++ Explainability
Gradient-weighted Class Activation Maps highlight exactly which joint regions drove the prediction — every pixel earns its attention score.
02
Attention Pooling
Learned per-slice attention weights let the model emphasise the most informative MRI slices and down-weight noisy frames automatically.
03
Multi-Plane Fusion
Independent ResNet18 encoders process axial, coronal and sagittal volumes; their representations are fused for a holistic view of the joint.
04
Quality Assurance
CLAHE preprocessing normalises intensity across scanners and field strengths, keeping prediction accuracy robust to imaging hardware variation.
05
Research Collaboration
Built on the Stanford MRNet dataset with AUC 0.954 on the held-out validation set, matching radiologist-level performance on ACL pathology.
